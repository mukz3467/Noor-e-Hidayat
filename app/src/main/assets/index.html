Arabic letters

Begin by learning the Arabic alphabet and correct letter sounds with a qualified teacher.

Makharij (articulation points)

Learn where each Arabic letter is pronounced. Listen, repeat slowly, and ask a qualified teacher to correct pronunciation.

Basic Tajweed

Study rules such as Noon Sakinah, Tanween, Meem Sakinah, Madd and Qalqalah with a qualified Quran teacher.

Daily practice

Set a small daily reading goal, revise regularly and focus on accurate pronunciation rather than speed.
